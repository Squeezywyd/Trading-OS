> **Key Point:** No checklist pass, no trade. This runs in the seconds between seeing a setup and clicking the order — it's the gate, not a study exercise. Grade the setup on **process**, before you know the outcome.

## How grading works

Every box below counts equally toward a percentage of the whole checklist — the app computes this live as you check items on the trade form. The point isn't to hit A+ every time; it's to know *honestly* which boxes you skipped, because that's the box that will explain the loss afterward. Note: if you edit this checklist (add/remove items) in the in-app editor, the percentages below shift automatically — the grade is always "% of *this* checklist checked," not a fixed item count.

## Context

- [ ] Daily bias written in Daily Prep, with an explicit invalidation price
- [ ] This trade's direction matches that bias
- [ ] Bias has not already been invalidated today

## Location

- [ ] Price is in discount of the relevant range for a long, or premium for a short
- [ ] Entry is not chasing price into the opposite-side liquidity pool

## Time

- [ ] Inside a killzone or a Silver Bullet window — not a dead window (late London, NY lunch)
- [ ] News times for today are known, and this entry isn't walking into an unscheduled release

## Trigger

- [ ] A defined liquidity level was actually swept (wick through it, not just approached)
- [ ] MSS with displacement followed the sweep — not just a slow drift back
- [ ] The displacement left behind a PD array (FVG, OB, breaker, or RB) to enter from

## Entry

- [ ] Entry at that PD array — OB, RB, FVG, or the 62-79% OTE band, ideally two or more overlapping
- [ ] Stop is beyond the sweep or the zone extreme, not an arbitrary point count
- [ ] Target is the first untouched liquidity pool in the draw direction
- [ ] Reward-to-risk is at least 2R to that first target before considering the trade

## Risk

- [ ] Size came from the sizing formula (risk $ ÷ (stop points × point value)), not a round number picked after the fact
- [ ] This trade is within today's max-trades and daily-stop rules
- [ ] Stop/target bracket is set at entry, not adjusted in after

## Grading

With the 17 items above, that works out to:

- **A+ — 100% (17/17):** every box true. Full size, per plan.
- **A — 80%+ (14-16/17, up to 3 missing):** standard size, but review exactly which boxes were skipped.
- **B — 60%+ (11-13/17, 4-6 missing):** half size at most — and treat a missing Risk-section box as a hard stop regardless of the overall percentage.
- **C — below 60% (10/17 or fewer):** do not take it. Log why in the journal instead — a skipped C-grade setup is still worth a line in Daily Prep.

A missing box in **Risk** is worth a second look no matter what the overall percentage says — an A-grade setup with no defined stop is not actually an A-grade setup.

## Common mistakes

- **Grading after the trade wins.** Grade before you know the outcome, or the checklist becomes a story you tell yourself after the fact instead of a filter.
- **Treating "close enough" as a pass.** A sweep that only approaches the level, or a stop set by feel instead of the zone extreme, is an unchecked box — not a technicality.
- **Skipping the Risk section because the setup "looks too good to miss."** The best-looking setups are exactly the ones oversized on tilt.
