> **Key Point:** No checklist pass, no trade. Grade the setup before you enter.

## Context
- [ ] Daily bias written with invalidation level
- [ ] Trade direction matches bias
- [ ] Price is in discount (long) or premium (short) of the relevant range

## Time
- [ ] Inside a killzone or Silver Bullet window
- [ ] Not in NY lunch
- [ ] News time known and accounted for

## Trigger
- [ ] A defined liquidity level was swept
- [ ] MSS with displacement afterwards
- [ ] FVG left behind by the displacement

## Entry
- [ ] Entry at OB / RB / FVG / OTE, ideally an overlap
- [ ] Stop beyond the sweep or zone extreme
- [ ] Target at the first untouched liquidity pool
- [ ] Reward to risk at least 2R to the first target

## Risk
- [ ] Size from the formula
- [ ] Within daily rules
- [ ] ATM/bracket set at entry

## Grading
- **A+:** every box true.
- **A:** one box missing that is not Context or Risk.
- **B:** two missing. Half size.
- **C:** do not take.
