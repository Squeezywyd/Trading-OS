> **Key Point:** Bias = which side of liquidity price is being drawn to today, plus the level that proves you wrong. It is not a prediction of every candle.

## Routine (run in this order)
1. **HTF draw on liquidity.** Weekly/Daily: nearest untouched pool (PWH/PWL, old highs/lows, equal highs/lows) and unfilled HTF PD arrays (FVG, OB).
2. **Location.** Premium or discount of the current Daily/Weekly range. Discount favors long, premium favors short.
3. **Previous-day logic.** Use the table below.
4. **Reaction check.** Did price reject from an HTF PD array (wick + close back) or close a body through it?
5. **Reference opens.** Where is price relative to Midnight Open, 8:30 Open, 9:30 Open? Bullish day = buy below the open. Bearish day = sell above the open.
6. **News.** List every high-impact release with time (ET). Mark the one you trade around.
7. **Expected AMD profile.** Which side gets manipulated first, and in which session.
8. **Write it down** in Daily Prep: bias, confidence, draw, invalidation, game plan.

## Previous-day behavior to bias

| Yesterday | Meaning | Bias today |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Closed above PDH with displacement | Buy-side accepted | Bullish continuation. Buy pullbacks into discount or FVG above PDH. Target next BSL. |
| Wicked above PDH, closed back below | Buy-side raid failed | Bearish. Target PDL then SSL. |
| Closed below PDL with displacement | Sell-side accepted | Bearish continuation. Sell rallies into premium or FVG below PDL. Target next SSL. |
| Wicked below PDL, closed back above | Sell-side raid failed | Bullish. Target PDH then BSL. |
| Inside day (neither PDH nor PDL taken) | Range, one side still to be raided | No bias until a raid happens. Trade the reversal after the sweep. |
| Outside day (both taken) | Expansion both ways, unclear | Lower confidence. Reduce size or skip. |

## Confidence
- **High:** draw, location, previous-day logic and news all point the same way.
- **Medium:** two of the three agree.
- **Low:** conflict. Half size or no trade.

## Invalidation rules
- Body close through the HTF PD array you are leaning on.
- Price takes the opposite-side liquidity first and displaces away from it before your entry window.
- Bias is void once invalidated. Do not re-argue it mid-session.

## Pre-session checklist
- [ ] HTF draw identified
- [ ] Premium/discount noted
- [ ] Previous-day row picked
- [ ] News times listed
- [ ] Invalidation level written
- [ ] Daily Prep entry created
