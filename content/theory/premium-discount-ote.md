> **Key Point:** Buy in discount, sell in premium. Equilibrium (50%) of the range divides them.

## How to measure
- Draw from the swing low to the swing high of the leg that broke structure (bullish), or high to low (bearish).
- **Equilibrium:** 50%.
- **Premium:** above 50%. **Discount:** below 50%.

## OTE (optimal trade entry)
- Retracement zone of **62% to 79%** of the leg. Sweet spot around 70.5%.
- Best entries sit where OTE overlaps an FVG, OB or RB.

## Targets (extensions of the leg)
- 0% (the swing high/low itself) = first target, usually a liquidity pool.
- Extension -1 and -2 = expansion targets when displacement is strong.
- Prefer liquidity pools over fixed numbers. Use extensions when no pool is near.

## Rules
- Long entries below 50% of the range, short entries above 50%.
- Entry deeper than 79% weakens the structure. Beyond 100% = leg invalid.
- Use the HTF range for bias, the LTF leg for entry.
- No PD array overlap in OTE = lower grade.
