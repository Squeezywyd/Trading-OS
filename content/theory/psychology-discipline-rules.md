> **Key Point:** Most losses come from breaking rules, not from the model. Track process first, P&L second.

## Pre-session state check
- [ ] Slept enough, no hangover from the last session
- [ ] Daily Prep done before the killzone
- [ ] Risk rules and daily stop written
- [ ] No open emotional trigger (argument, money stress)

Any box unchecked: half size or no trade.

## Tilt triggers (stop trading)
- Two losses in a row: 15 minute break, then re-read bias.
- Loss that hits your daily stop: done for the day.
- Entering without a sweep + MSS.
- Sizing up after a loss.
- Moving or removing a stop.

## Emotion tags to use in the journal
Calm, Confident, FOMO, Revenge, Fear, Hesitant, Overconfident. Tag honestly. The weekly recap groups by emotion.

## Post-trade loop
1. Screenshot the setup before entry and after exit.
2. Grade the setup (A+ to C) **before** looking at P&L.
3. Answer: followed plan? Bias aligned? What was the one mistake?
4. Write one Lesson line.

## Weekly loop
- Count rule breaks. Target 0.
- Compare A+ trade expectancy to B/C. Cut the B/C models that lose.
- Pick one focus for next week.
