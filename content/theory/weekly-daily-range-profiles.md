> **Key Point:** The week has the same phases as the day. Highs and lows of the week tend to form early (Mon to Wed) and the real move develops mid-week.

## Weekly templates

| Profile | Structure | Trade |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Classic expansion | Mon-Tue consolidation/manipulation, Wed-Thu expansion, Fri retrace | Wait for the Mon/Tue sweep, trade Wed/Thu in bias direction |
| Midweek reversal | Trend Mon-Tue, reversal Wed at a weekly PD array or liquidity level | Sweep + MSS Wednesday, target opposite side |
| Consolidation reversal | Range all week, break and reverse Thu-Fri | Reduce size, fewer entries until the break |
| Trend week | Monday sets direction, no clean reversal | Buy/sell pullbacks with bias all week |

## Daily behavior
- London often sets the high or low of the day.
- NY AM distribution moves away from the London extreme toward the draw.
- If London is a trend, NY continues or retraces to the London FVG before continuing.
- If London consolidates, NY takes one side and reverses.

## Rules
- Weekly high/low usually forms by Wednesday. After that, expect the opposite side of the week to be tested.
- Friday: reduced size after NY AM, retracements are common.
- Log the weekly profile in Weekly Recaps. Compare to what happened.
