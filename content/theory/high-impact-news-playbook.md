> **Key Point:** News is a liquidity event. The first spike takes stops, the second move is the tradable one.

## Calendar reference (ET)

| Event | Time | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CPI, PPI, NFP, Jobless Claims, Retail Sales | 08:30 | Highest impact at the data release. Lands before the 9:30 cash open. |
| JOLTS, ISM, Consumer Confidence, some Fed speakers | 10:00 | Mid-tier. Basis of the 10am model. |
| FOMC statement | 14:00 | Major. Wide range and whipsaws. |
| FOMC press conference | 14:30 | Second wave, often reverses the statement reaction. |
| Fed chair testimony / speeches | varies | Check the calendar the day before. |

Zurich time is ET plus 6 hours (5 hours in the daylight-saving mismatch weeks).

## Approach
1. **Before:** mark the pre-news range, session high/low, nearest OB/FVG. Set bias and skip conditions.
2. **At release:** do not enter on the print candle. Wait for the first spike to sweep a level.
3. **After the spike:** wait for MSS with displacement and an FVG.
4. **Entry:** retrace into FVG / RB / news-candle open.
5. **Stop:** beyond the spike extreme. **Target:** opposite range side.

## Rules
- Spreads and slippage widen. Use limit entries, cut size if the stop exceeds your cap.
- Confirm your account rules allow news trading before you rely on it. (LucidFlex allows it. LucidDaily treats it as a hard breach.)
- Skip when the daily bias is Low confidence and news is at 8:30, unless the model explicitly covers it.
- Log the event, surprise vs forecast, and reaction in Daily Prep.
