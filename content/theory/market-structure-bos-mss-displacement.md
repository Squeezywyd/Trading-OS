> **Key Point:** A break only counts if it has displacement. A wick through a level is a sweep, not a break.

## Definitions
- **Swing high:** candle with a lower high on each side. **Swing low:** candle with a higher low on each side.
- **Short-term / intermediate / long-term:** a swing with lower-order swings on both sides is the next order up. Use the order that matches your entry timeframe.
- **Bullish structure:** higher highs and higher lows. **Bearish:** lower lows and lower highs.

## Breaks

| Term | Rule | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BOS (break of structure) | Body closes beyond the last swing **in trend direction** | Continuation. Look for pullback entries. |
| MSS (market structure shift) | Body closes beyond the swing that produced the last extreme, **against** trend, with displacement | Reversal signal. Look for retrace entry into the FVG/OB it left. |
| Sweep | Wick beyond a level, body closes back | Liquidity taken. Not a structure break. |

CHoCH in SMC language = first MSS against the trend.

## Displacement
Strong, one-directional move with large bodies and small wicks that leaves an **FVG**. Without an FVG or clear body dominance it does not qualify.

## Entry flow
1. Liquidity sweep.
2. MSS with displacement.
3. Retrace into FVG / OB / OTE.
4. Stop beyond the sweep extreme. Target next liquidity.

## Rules
- Higher-timeframe structure sets the direction. Lower-timeframe MSS times the entry.
- MSS with no prior sweep is lower grade.
- Internal structure (inside the current leg) confirms entry. External structure (the range extremes) defines targets.
- Structure is invalid the moment price body-closes beyond the last protected swing.
